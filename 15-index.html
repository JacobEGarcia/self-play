WHAT YOU ARE WATCHING

Two tabular Q-learning agents play each other at high speed in the background, with sparring rounds against a scripted drone every third game. Up front, the current champion defends the left court against that drone in real time. At first it loses nearly every point. Give it a minute.

HOW IT LEARNS

The court is discretized into 900 states: ball position, heading, and paddle offset. Three actions: up, hold, down. Reward is +1 for a point, -1 for a concession, +0.2 per return, plus a small shaping reward for closing on the ball. The champion is a checkpoint: a challenger must beat it in a seeded 41-game evaluation to take the crown, so the amber win line cannot go down. No neural network, no weights, no server. The whole brain is a table of 2,700 numbers, updated and saved in your browser.

WHY SELF-PLAY

This is the loop from the Stanford RL lecture: skill bootstrapped by playing against yourself, with zero human examples. AlphaGo reported the same result at scale - the self-play version beat the one trained on human games. The amber line is the champion's win rate against the drone. Watch it climb.
